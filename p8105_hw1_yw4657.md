The `penguins` dataset contains data on penguins from the Palmer
Archipelago. Important variables include `species`, `island`,
`bill_length_mm`, `bill_depth_mm`, `flipper_length_mm`, `body_mass_g`,
`sex`, and `year`. The dataset contains 344 observations and 8
variables. The mean flipper length is 200.92 mm.
